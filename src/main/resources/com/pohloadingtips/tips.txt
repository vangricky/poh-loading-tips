# POH Loading Tips - one tip per line:  KIND|Category|Text
# KIND is TIP (shown as "Quick Tip") or FACT (shown as "Did you know?").
# Keep each one to 1-2 short sentences: the loading screen is only up for a second or two.
# Facts were checked against the Old School RuneScape Wiki (oldschool.runescape.wiki), November 2026.

# ---------- Player-owned house ----------
TIP|House|Turn off "Teleport Inside" in House Options and your house teleports will drop you outside the portal instead.
TIP|House|An ornate rejuvenation pool (90 Construction) restores HP, prayer, run energy, special attack and stats, and cures poison and venom.
TIP|House|An ornate jewellery box (91 Construction) gives you free glory, dueling, games, skills, combat and wealth teleports.
TIP|House|An occult altar (90 Construction) lets you swap between the standard, Ancient, Lunar and Arceuus spellbooks whenever you like.
TIP|House|A spirit tree & fairy ring (95 Construction, 83 Farming) puts two whole teleport networks in your garden.
TIP|House|A demon butler (50 Construction) carries 26 items per trip, which works out to about 1,250 coins each time.
TIP|House|Dying inside a player-owned house is safe: you're sent outside with all your items and your stats restored.
TIP|House|Poisoned? Leaving your house in any way removes the poison.
TIP|House|Switch building mode on in Settings while you're outside, and your next house teleport puts you straight into building mode.
TIP|House|Your house can move to Prifddinas for 50,000 coins once you have 70 Construction and have finished Song of the Elves.
TIP|House|Your room limit grows with your level: 24 rooms at 1 Construction, up to 38 rooms at 99.
TIP|House|House Options has a setting that hides every door in your house, so you can walk straight through.
TIP|Construction|Mahogany Homes contracts give great Construction XP, and their points buy the carpenter's outfit for bonus XP.

# ---------- Prayer ----------
TIP|Prayer|A gilded altar gives 350% Prayer XP per bone with both incense burners lit, compared with 250% when they're unlit.
TIP|Prayer|Phials, in the Rimmington general store, un-notes items for 5 coins each. That's right beside the house portal.
TIP|Prayer|The ectophial gives you unlimited teleports to the Ectofuntus in Port Phasmatys.

# ---------- Teleports & travel ----------
TIP|Teleports|The ring of wealth teleports you to Miscellania, the Grand Exchange, Falador Park and Dondakan's Rock.
TIP|Teleports|A games necklace can take you to Wintertodt Camp, the Chasm of Tears, Corporeal Beast, Burthorpe or the Barbarian Outpost.
TIP|Teleports|A skills necklace teleports you to six guilds: Fishing, Mining, Crafting, Cooks', Woodcutting and Farming.
TIP|Teleports|Finish the Lumbridge & Draynor Elite Diary and you can use fairy rings without a dramen staff.
TIP|Teleports|After the Varrock Medium Diary, Varrock Teleport can take you to the Grand Exchange instead.
TIP|Wilderness|Most teleports stop working above level 20 Wilderness, but glories and the ring of life work up to level 30.
TIP|Wilderness|A looting bag holds 28 extra items, but you can only put items in it while you're in the Wilderness.

# ---------- Skilling ----------
TIP|Skills|Finish the Varrock Museum's natural history quiz for 1,000 Hunter XP, 1,000 Slayer XP and 28 kudos.
TIP|Skills|Tears of Guthix gives you XP in your lowest skill once a week. Don't forget it!
TIP|Agility|A full graceful outfit makes run energy recover 30% faster.
TIP|Agility|A stamina potion cuts run energy drain by 70% for two minutes per dose.
TIP|Thieving|A dodgy necklace gives you a 25% chance to dodge the stun when a pickpocket fails.
TIP|Thieving|The full rogue outfit can give you double loot when pickpocketing.
TIP|Herblore|An amulet of chemistry gives a 5% chance to make a 4-dose potion instead of a 3-dose one.
TIP|Farming|Magic secateurs give you 10% more herbs, allotment crops and hops.
TIP|Cooking|Cooking gauntlets from Family Crest lower your burn chance on lobsters, swordfish, sharks and more.
TIP|Smithing|Goldsmith gauntlets more than double the XP for smelting gold bars, from 22.5 to 56.2.
TIP|Smithing|Ice gloves let you pick up bars from the Blast Furnace without cooling them first.
TIP|Mining|Wearing a charged amulet of glory roughly triples your chance of finding gems while mining.
TIP|Daily|After the Varrock Easy Diary, Zaff sells you discounted battlestaves every day.

# ---------- Storage & interface ----------
TIP|Interface|Turn on "Shift click to drop items" in Settings and you can empty your inventory in seconds.
TIP|Interface|Hold the middle mouse button and drag to rotate the camera quickly.
TIP|Storage|A gem bag holds up to 60 of each uncut gem.
TIP|Storage|A rune pouch holds three types of runes in one slot. A divine rune pouch holds four.
TIP|Combat|Duke Horacio in Lumbridge Castle hands out anti-dragon shields for free.
TIP|Magic|The Fountain of Rune in level 47-48 Wilderness gives you unlimited runes for spells cast nearby, but you get no XP.

# ---------- Did you know? ----------
FACT|History|Old School RuneScape is built from a backup of the game as it was on 10 August 2007. It launched on 22 February 2013.
FACT|History|The Grand Exchange wasn't in OSRS at launch. Its poll passed with just 76.3% in 2015, barely clearing the 75% it needed.
FACT|History|Free-to-play OSRS only arrived on 19 February 2015, after the poll for it passed with 85.9% of the vote.
FACT|History|The 2013 poll to bring back Old School fell short of the 500,000 votes Jagex asked for, but they kept it free for members anyway.
FACT|History|OSRS on mobile launched on 31 October 2018.
FACT|History|OSRS hit its all-time peak of 257,491 players online on 1 September 2025.
FACT|House|Player-owned houses first came to RuneScape on 31 May 2006, along with the gilded altar.
FACT|House|In the lore, houses sit in pocket dimensions made by wizards who folded space to fit lots of plots into a tiny area.
FACT|House|Since a January 2024 update, every house loads in the same time, no matter how big it is.
FACT|House|When you call the demon butler, he says "What is thy bidding, my master?", a nod to Darth Vader.
FACT|House|Ironmen can't enter other players' houses unless they're in the same Group Ironman group.
FACT|NPCs|Hans in Lumbridge Castle can tell you how old your account is and exactly how long you've played.
FACT|NPCs|Hans walks a lap of Lumbridge Castle in about a minute, like a clock's second hand. His name may be a pun on "hands".
FACT|NPCs|Jagex has said Hans was RuneScape's first NPC, but Bob shows up earlier in RuneScape Classic's data files.
FACT|NPCs|Phials' name follows the old Certer naming pattern, like Niles, Miles and Giles. It's a nod to the note-swapping NPCs of RuneScape Classic.
FACT|Bugs|The crystal crown in the Prifddinas clothing shop restocks only once every 250 million game ticks. That's about 4.8 years!
FACT|Bugs|Fishing Trawler's slow XP comes from an accidental 2.5x nerf during the switch from Classic to RS2. Nobody noticed for about 20 years.
FACT|Bugs|Thanks to a copy-paste mistake, crows meant for Tirannwn and Zeah teleport to the Lumbridge cow field after every server restart.
FACT|Bugs|For a while, an integer overflow bug meant Dorgesh-Kaan goblins would accept billion-coin offers for cheap food.
FACT|Bugs|A mistake on the wiki kept the black shield (h2) trading at about 10 times its real value on the GE for three years.
FACT|Bugs|In 2003, code from Gertrude's Cat accidentally broke desert heat in RuneScape Classic, and it never worked again.
FACT|Secrets|Time, Reality and Illusion runes were in RuneScape's code in 2001, but they were removed before anyone could get them.
FACT|Secrets|You can earn exactly 1 XP in every skill except Runecrafting, Firemaking and Prayer.
FACT|Secrets|Jagex once planned for every monster to block your path like the gorillas on Ape Atoll, until they were talked out of it.
FACT|Secrets|Each glory charge from the Fountain of Rune has a 1 in 25,000 chance to turn it into an amulet of eternal glory with unlimited charges.
FACT|Secrets|The Fountain of Rune recharges glories to six charges instead of the usual four.
FACT|Secrets|The Fountain of Rune was built from the Obelisk of Water, a magic tree texture and old rune models, because dev tools were limited in 2014.
FACT|Grand Exchange|Part of the Grand Exchange tax pays for Probita's pet insurance, which is why it's now free.
FACT|Grand Exchange|On Android, the GE's custom-amount keypad has a minus key that does nothing.
FACT|Grand Exchange|The little jingle when an offer completes is the opening of the music track The Trade Parade.

# ---------- Training tips ----------
TIP|Prayer|Bones offered at the Ectofuntus give 400% Prayer XP, more than a gilded altar.
TIP|Prayer|The Chaos Altar in level 38 Wilderness gives 350% Prayer XP and has a 50% chance to save each bone.
TIP|Prayer|Right-click the Prayer orb next to your minimap to set up quick-prayers you can turn on with one click.
TIP|Quests|Waterfall Quest has no skill requirements and gives 13,750 Attack and Strength XP, enough to take a new account from level 1 to 30.
TIP|Mining|Inside the Mining Guild's members' area you get an invisible +7 Mining boost.
TIP|Fishing|Barbarian fishing gives you Strength and Agility XP on top of your Fishing XP.
TIP|Cooking|Cook on a range instead of a fire. Ranges burn less food, and the Lumbridge Castle range burns even less after Cook's Assistant.
TIP|Cooking|The Hosidius kitchen range lowers your burn chance by 5%, or by 10% after the Kourend & Kebos Hard Diary.
TIP|Farming|Use your harvested crops on a tool leprechaun and they'll be noted for free.
TIP|Farming|Pay a nearby farmer to look after your trees and they will never die from disease.
TIP|Agility|You need 260 marks of grace for a full graceful outfit.
TIP|Magic|High Level Alchemy gives you 60% of an item's value in coins. Low Level Alchemy only gives 40%.
TIP|Runecrafting|Essence pouches hold 3, 6, 9 and 12 essence. The colossal pouch from Guardians of the Rift holds 40!
TIP|Skilling|Skilling outfits like the angler, lumberjack, prospector and pyromancer give 2.5% bonus XP when you wear the full set.

TIP|Combat|In PvP, protection prayers only block 40% of the damage instead of all of it.
TIP|Quests|Animal Magnetism rewards Ava's device, which saves and picks up your arrows while you fight.
TIP|Quests|Recipe for Disaster unlocks barrows gloves, some of the best gloves in the game.
TIP|Quests|Monkey Madness I lets you buy and wield the dragon scimitar.
